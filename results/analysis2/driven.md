### What I changed, and why
1. **Peak precision at h = 1e-5 was overstated.** The χ_rep/ρ peak is a single grid point. Both neighbours (2.39 at g = 0.251, 3.57 at g = 0.362) are below half height. The CI [0.293, 0.315] comes only from a 3-point parabola, because the bootstrap argmax never leaves g = 0.3016, so it ignores the grid spacing (factor 1.20, 0.080 decades). The honest bracket is the grid cell [0.275, 0.331]. The reported FWHM of 0.09 decades is an interpolation artefact; the width is unresolved (< 0.16 decades). The conclusion survives at that precision: P5 with measured τ (0.305, or 0.317 at ν·τ = 2) falls in the peak's grid cell, and P5 with theory τ (0.448) lies two grid points higher. **Fixed in the generator:** `find_peak` now returns `g_cell`, `single_point` and `max_above_window`. The headline json carries `sim_F_rep_grid_cell`, `sim_F_rep_single_point`, `sim_F_rep_peak_value` and `sim_F_rep_max_above_window`. The Summary bullets and the table's FWHM column say this, and panel (f)'s bar for the peak is now the bootstrap CI widened to the grid cell. The script was rerun with the same seed, and every pre-existing json value is unchanged.
2. **The h = 1e-4 "resolved" peak is only a local maximum.** At g = 0.3016 the value is 1.94, but χ_rep/ρ reaches 4.76 at g = 9.95, above the 0.1–1.0 search window. The bootstrap argmax falls on 0.3016 (79%), 0.2509 (15%) or 0.4358 (6%). Only the h = 1e-5 peak (28.7) is a global maximum; the largest value above the window there is 4.18. The generator now says so in the Summary, and panel (b)'s title now says the ▼ means "resolved in the shaded window".
3. **"Lifetimes underestimated about 29-fold" needed its range.** 29.2 is the geometric mean of τ_sim/τ_theory over g = 0.285–0.375, restricted to τ_sim ≥ 200, with a range of 8.7–226. The ratio rises steeply with g. At g = 0.30, where the h = 1e-5 comparison sits, it is 8.7–10.4; at g = 0.26–0.28 it is 3.8–6.5. The generator's sentence now says this.
4. **Units error, agent's returned summary only.** "The 1/(α − λ) shape of the leading mode accounts for only 0.005 to 0.025 of the excess density" is wrong. 0.005–0.025 are values of ρ/ρ0 − 1, not fractions. As a fraction of the h → 0 first-order excess (B only) the pole is 6.3%, 8.7% and 17% at g/g_lin = 0.61, 0.74 and 0.89. As a fraction of the simulated excess at h = 1e-5 it is 4.7–11%. The qualitative point stands: the leading mode is a minor part of the sub-threshold response.
5. **"The exact first-order response fits the simulation" (agent summary) was selective.** The χ² values over 3 points, with p-values, are:
   - h = 1e-5: 1.35 (p 0.72).
   - h = 1e-4: 9.0 (p 0.029), rejected at 5%; B only is worse at 16.2.
   - h = 1e-3: 7.2 (p 0.066).
   - h = 1e-2: 0.40 (p 0.94).

   The evidence that drive switches inhibition back on comes from h ≥ 1e-3, where B only gives χ² of 384 and 3762. At h = 1e-5 the two variants are indistinguishable.
6. **QMF agreement (agent summary) was stated as a bound when it is a median.** Below g_lin the medians are 1.00–1.09, but single points deviate by up to 21% (h = 1e-5, g = 0.1445: ratio 1.21) and by ≤ 8% for h ≥ 1e-4. At h = 0.1 for 0.5 ≤ g ≤ 3, the median is 1.04 but the points span 1.005–1.143 (up to 14%), not "within 4%". The explanation "because QMF overstates inhibition" for the factor 2.2–2.3 shortfall at g = 0.5–3 is not tested in this analysis. It is a hypothesis, consistent with the earlier finding that the QMF D line is too inhibitory (STATE.md, 23:40).
7. **The module size needed at h = 1e-4 depends on the threshold.** With the agent's threshold I reproduce n = 127 [74, 198] at g = 0.251 and 246 [150, 439] at g = 0.302. With a threshold that does not use A(g), n_off + 6√(n_off + 1):
   - g = 0.251: n = 78 [47, 118].
   - g = 0.302: n = 313 [199, 641], which includes 526.

   So "n = 526 lies outside" holds robustly only at g = 0.25, and there the measured h = 0 lifetime (68) is dominated by the initial transient (the agent's caveat 4). At h = 1e-5, g = 0.302, the needed n is 310–380 under either threshold, with intervals of about 63–930.

10. **The downward shift is robust in sign; its size is approximate.** At the same g = 0.3016, p_on is 0.83 [0.74, 0.91] at h = 1e-4 against 0.39 [0.20, 0.58] at h = 1e-5 (noise-only threshold); with the agent's threshold it is 0.79 against 0.35.
